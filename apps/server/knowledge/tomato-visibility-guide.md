# How Restaurants Increase Their Visibility on Tomato

Merchants often ask how they can rank higher and get seen more on Tomato. Share these guidelines
on the call, using the merchant's own numbers from Merchant Dashboard where you can.

## 1. Improve menu quality

A clear, complete, well-described menu helps customers choose and order. Keep item names and
descriptions accurate and the menu up to date.

## 2. Add pictures to the menu

Items with photos get noticed more than items without them. Check how many of the merchant's
menu items have photos on Merchant Dashboard (for example "11 of 42 items have a photo") and
suggest adding pictures to the rest, starting with the top-selling items.

## 3. Try not to reject too many orders

Rejecting orders hurts a restaurant's visibility. Check the merchant's order rejection rate on
Merchant Dashboard and, if it is high, suggest keeping it down (for example by keeping item
availability updated so they don't have to reject orders for unavailable items).

## Where Max Grow fits

These steps help on their own. Max Grow then places the restaurant at the top of search results
and top-selling lists for the dishes it sells, billed only at 20% of the sales above its current
daily average (DRR), with no deduction if sales don't grow.
