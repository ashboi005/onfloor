# Tomato Max Grow: Sales Playbook

Internal reference for Tomato's merchant sales team pitching Max Grow to restaurant partners.
This is the source of truth for how Max Grow works and how it is billed. If something is not in
here, do not promise it: say you will confirm and follow up.

## What Max Grow does

Max Grow increases a restaurant's visibility on Tomato. A participating restaurant is placed at
the top of search results and of the top-selling restaurant lists for the dishes it sells.

Example: when a customer searches for "chhole bhature" on Tomato, a Max Grow restaurant that sells
chhole bhature appears at the top of the results. More visibility means more impressions, and
more impressions mean more orders.

## How billing works (incremental only)

Max Grow is billed entirely on incremental sales.

- **Baseline: the DRR.** DRR stands for Daily Run Rate: the merchant's average daily sales before
  the campaign. The DRR is shown in the Nugget ticket for every merchant.
- **Incremental sales** are the sales above the DRR. If a merchant's DRR is ₹100 and their daily
  sales rise to an average of ₹200, the incremental sales are ₹100.
- **Commission: 20% of the incremental value only.** In the example, 20% of ₹100 = ₹20.
- **No risk.** If sales stay equal to or below the baseline DRR, there is **zero deduction**.
- **Billing:** deductions are billed **weekly** and settled **directly from the merchant's Tomato
  payout**. There is nothing to pay upfront and no separate invoice.
- **The commission is non-negotiable.** It is 20% for every partner. Never offer a lower rate,
  a discount or a waiver.
- **The merchant can stop Max Grow whenever they want.**
- **Minimum campaign duration: one month.** Set the start and end dates in Ad Studio and
  dictate them clearly to the merchant.

The merchant's existing sales (up to the DRR) are never charged. Only the extra sales that
Max Grow helps bring in are.

## Worked examples (use the merchant's own DRR from Nugget)

| DRR (₹/day) | Average daily sales during the campaign | Incremental (₹/day) | Commission per day (20%) | Per week (7 days) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 200 | 100 | 20 | 140 |
| 4,300 | 5,300 | 1,000 | 200 | 1,400 |
| 9,800 | 12,800 | 3,000 | 600 | 4,200 |
| 14,200 | 18,200 | 4,000 | 800 | 5,600 |
| 22,800 | 27,800 | 5,000 | 1,000 | 7,000 |
| 38,500 | 44,500 | 6,000 | 1,200 | 8,400 |
| 14,200 | 14,000 | 0 | 0 | 0 (no deduction) |

The merchant always keeps 80% of every extra rupee. When sales don't go above the DRR, nothing is
deducted at all.

## Answering common questions

**"How much will you cut?"**
20%, and only on the extra sales above your current daily average (your DRR). "Sir, your DRR is
₹14,200. If your daily sales go to ₹18,200, the extra is ₹4,000, and 20% of that is ₹800 a day."

**"What if my sales don't increase?"**
Then nothing is deducted. "If your sales stay at or below ₹14,200 a day, there is zero deduction."

**"When and how do you deduct it?"**
Weekly, settled directly from the Tomato payout. Nothing upfront.

**"Can you make it 10% / 15%?" / "My friend got a lower rate."**
The commission is non-negotiable and the same for every partner. "The 20% is fixed for everyone,
sir, and it only ever applies to sales you wouldn't have had otherwise. On sales you already make,
you pay nothing."

**"Can I stop it in between?"**
Yes. The merchant can stop Max Grow whenever they want.

**"Why should I pay on my own sales?"**
They don't. The DRR is the baseline; only sales above it are incremental and only those are
charged.

**"How do I increase my visibility on Tomato?"**
See the visibility guide: improve menu quality, add pictures to the menu, and try not to reject
too many orders. Max Grow then puts the restaurant at the top of search and top-selling lists.

## Things never to say

- Any commission other than 20% (for example 15% or 30%).
- That the commission applies to total sales.
- That there is a deduction when sales don't grow.
- That the rate can be negotiated or discounted.
- That the merchant is locked in and can't stop.
- That payment is upfront or invoiced separately.
