# Tomato Live Call Guide: Max Grow Policy and Approved Answers

Company policy for Tomato's merchant sales agents on live calls with restaurant partners, and the
approved answer to every question merchants usually ask about Max Grow. The live copilot reads this
document; every number and promise an agent makes on a call must match it. If something is not in
here, do not promise it: say you will confirm and follow up.

## Company policy at a glance

- Max Grow places the restaurant at the top of search results and top-selling lists for the dishes it sells.
- Commission is exactly 20%, charged only on incremental sales: sales above the merchant's DRR.
- DRR (Daily Run Rate) is the merchant's average daily sales before the campaign. It is the baseline.
- If sales stay equal to or below the DRR, there is zero deduction.
- Deductions are billed weekly and settled directly from the merchant's Tomato payout. Nothing upfront.
- The commission is non-negotiable. No discounts, no lower rate, no waiver, for any partner.
- The merchant can stop Max Grow whenever they want.
- The minimum campaign duration is one month; dictate the exact start and end dates.
- Only pitch to the owner or someone who can make financial decisions; otherwise book a callback.
- If the merchant complains, show empathy before anything else.

## Opening the call (standard lines)

"Good morning, sir. My name is (your name). I am calling from the Tomato team." Then confirm the
merchant's name and restaurant name, and verify they are the owner. If someone else answered
(a manager or family member), ask: "Are you the right person to make financial decisions for this
restaurant?" If yes, proceed. If no, ask when the owner will be available and schedule a callback;
if they don't know, pick an appropriate callback slot yourself.

## "Why are you calling?" / "Kya offer hai?" / "What is this about?"

Approved answer: "Sir, we have a plan called Max Grow. It puts your restaurant at the top of search
and the top-selling list on Tomato for the dishes you sell, so more customers see you and order."
Example: when a customer searches for chhole bhature, a Max Grow restaurant selling chhole bhature
appears at the top.

## "How much will you cut?" / "Kitna katega?" / "What is the commission?" / "How much do you charge?"

Approved answer: "Only 20%, and only on the extra sales above your current daily average, your DRR.
Your existing sales are never charged." Worked example: DRR ₹100 a day. If daily sales rise to
₹200, the incremental sales are ₹100, and 20% of that is ₹20 a day. The merchant keeps the other
₹80 of the extra sales. If sales don't grow above the DRR, nothing is deducted.

Never say: 15%, 30% or any rate other than 20%; that it is charged on total sales.

## "What if my sales don't increase?" / "Agar sales nahi badhi toh bhi paisa katega?" / "Koi risk toh nahi?" / "Is there any risk?" / "Do I still pay if nothing changes?"

Approved answer: "Then nothing is deducted, sir. If your sales stay at or below your DRR, there is
zero deduction. You only pay 20% on the extra sales Max Grow brings in." There are no minimum
charges.

## "Can you make it 10%?" / "Das percent karo" / "My friend got it cheaper" / "Give me a discount"

Policy: the commission is non-negotiable and the same 20% for every partner. Never offer a discount,
a lower commission or a waiver, even to close the deal.

Approved answer: "Sir, 20% is the same for every partner and I can't change it. But remember, it is
only on the extra sales above your current average. On the sales you already make, you pay nothing."

## "When is it deducted?" / "Kab katega?" / "Do I pay upfront?" / "Will I get an invoice?"

Approved answer: "It is billed weekly and settled directly from your Tomato payout. There is nothing
to pay upfront and no separate invoice."

## "Can I stop it?" / "Beech mein band kar sakte hain?" / "Is there a lock-in?"

Approved answer: "Yes, you can stop Max Grow whenever you want, sir." The campaign itself is set up
for at least one month, with the start and end dates dictated on the call.

## "How do I increase my visibility?" / "Search mein upar kaise aayenge?" / "Why do new places rank above me?"

Approved answer, three guidelines:
1. Improve menu quality: clear, complete and up-to-date items and descriptions.
2. Add pictures to the menu, starting with the top-selling items.
3. Try not to reject too many orders.
Then: "Max Grow puts you at the top of search and top-selling lists on top of that."

## "Will you guarantee more orders?" / "Pakka orders badhenge?"

Policy: never guarantee orders or sales. Max Grow increases visibility.

Approved answer: "I can't promise a number, sir, but you only pay if your sales actually grow above
your DRR. If they don't, nothing is deducted."

## The merchant is complaining / "Koi sunta nahi" / "Last time ads were a waste"

Policy: show empathy and sympathy before anything else.

Approved lines: "I am really sorry to hear that." "I can understand your concern." "I will definitely
help you out of this problem." Then connect it to the no-risk point: "That's why with Max Grow, if
your sales don't grow, nothing is deducted."

## The person is not the owner / "Main manager hoon" / "Papa decide karte hain"

Ask: "Are you the right person to make financial decisions for this restaurant?" If not, do not
pitch or set anything up. Ask when the owner will be available and schedule a callback; if they
don't know, propose a slot yourself (for example tomorrow at 11 AM) and confirm it.

## The merchant agrees / "Theek hai, laga do, campaign start karo" / "Let's do it" / "Go ahead, set it up" / closing the sale and dictating the campaign dates

Set the campaign up (UID, email, Max Grow, start and end dates, minimum one month) and dictate the
timeline clearly: "Your campaign starts today, October 10th, and runs through November 10th for one
month." Then close: "Thank you for selecting our plan. Is there anything else I can help you with
today?"

## "Send me the details" / "Let me think" / "Email kar do"

Respect it. Summarise the two facts that matter ("20% only on sales above your current average,
nothing if sales don't grow") and send the follow-up email after the call. If they want a call later,
agree a date and time and book the callback.

## After the call

Within the 90-second disposition window in Nugget, send the matching email template (accepted:
campaign acceptance; rejected: rejection; not interested or not sure: follow-up; callback: callback
with the agreed date and time) and dispose the call.

## What QA penalises

- Misrepresenting plan terms (any rate other than 20%, commission on total sales, a deduction
  without growth, a negotiable rate, a lock-in, billing other than weekly from the payout).
- Skipping or incorrectly delivering the standard opening or closing lines.
- Not disposing the call within the allocated time.
