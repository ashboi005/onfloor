# OnFloor Ads: Sales Playbook (merchant acquisition)

Internal reference for agents selling OnFloor Ads to restaurants, cafés and cloud kitchens.
This is the source of truth for prices, guarantees and policies. If a number is not in here,
do not quote it: say you will confirm and follow up.

## Plans and pricing

OnFloor Ads has three plans. Every plan runs on the merchant's own monthly ad budget
(minimum ₹5,000, maximum ₹5,00,000), which the merchant can change at any time.

- **Basic**: listing in local search and the OnFloor app feed. No reach guarantee. Platform
  commission 12% on orders that come through OnFloor.
- **Premium**: everything in Basic, plus a **guaranteed reach of 12,000 local diners per month
  within a 5 km radius**, featured placement, and the **Weekend Boost** (priority placement
  Friday to Sunday). Platform commission 12%. Premium needs a monthly budget of at least ₹40,000.
- **Premium Plus**: everything in Premium, with guaranteed reach of 25,000 diners per month
  within 8 km, a dedicated account manager and a monthly performance review. Needs at least
  ₹90,000 a month.

Reach is the only thing OnFloor guarantees. Never guarantee orders, footfall or revenue.

## Discounts and approvals

- An agent may offer **up to 10% off the plan fee for the first three months** without
  approval.
- Anything above 10%, or any discount longer than three months, needs **manager approval
  before it is offered**. Do not say "I can do 15%" and ask later.
- Never discount the 12% commission. It is fixed for every plan.
- Approved phrasing: "I can't go beyond 10% on my own, but let me show you why Premium pays
  for itself before we talk discounts."

## Activation and timelines

- **Standard activation is 3 working days** from the signed upgrade.
- **Priority activation (2 working days)** is free for Premium Plus and can be requested for
  Premium.
- **Next-day activation is not standard and needs manager approval.** Never promise it on the
  call. Approved phrasing: "Standard activation is 3 working days. I'll flag it as priority and
  confirm a date with you today."
- Weekend Boost starts on the first Friday after activation.

## Compliance and disclosures

- At the start of every call, say that the call may be recorded for quality purposes.
- Never share another merchant's numbers or name them as a reference without their consent.
- Never promise a refund. Refunds are handled by the billing team within 7 working days.
- Account changes (budget, campaign status, notifications, radius, categories) must be made in
  the portal during the call, and confirmed back to the merchant out loud.

## Competitor facts

- **Zestify** advertises an "8% commission", but adds a **6% listing fee** on every order, so
  the real total is **about 14%**, against OnFloor's flat 12%. Zestify does not guarantee reach.
- **BiteBoost** charges no commission but a fixed ₹55,000 a month regardless of results, with no
  reach guarantee and no weekend placement.
- When a merchant quotes a competitor rate, ask what is included. Headline rates usually leave
  out listing or platform fees.

## Objection handling playbook

- **"Your commission is too high."** Don't defend the rate. Compare the all-in cost (Zestify is
  about 14% all-in against our 12%) and pivot to the reach guarantee: 12,000 local diners a
  month within 5 km on Premium.
- **"Competitor X is cheaper."** Ask what is included, then compare like for like: listing fees,
  guaranteed reach, weekend placement.
- **"What's the guarantee?"** Reach is guaranteed, not orders. Explain the 12,000-diner monthly
  reach and that it is reported in the portal every week.
- **"I need it live tomorrow."** Standard is 3 working days. Offer priority (2 days) and say you
  will confirm a date today. Do not promise next-day.
- **"Give me 20% off."** Hold at 10% for three months, and show the value first. If the merchant
  will sign today at a higher discount, say you will check with your manager and call back
  within the hour. Never offer it yourself.
- **"I don't have time."** Lead with one number: what Premium guarantees and what it costs for
  their budget. Then ask one question.

## Upsell and closing signals

- Merchant mentions **weekends being slow** → Premium's Weekend Boost.
- Merchant runs **more than one outlet** or spends over ₹90,000 a month → Premium Plus with an
  account manager.
- Merchant says "send me the details", "okay", "theek hai" or agrees on price → close: confirm
  the plan, the start date and the budget while they are on the line.

## Tone

Short answers, numbers first, one question at a time. Code-switching into Hindi or Hinglish
when the merchant does is encouraged. Let the merchant finish; don't talk over them.
